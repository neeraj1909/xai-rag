# Rejected experimental grounding candidate

**Status: rejected for integration; not active.** This directory preserves a
bounded experiment for later review. Do not apply the patch as a verified fix.
The production verifier was restored after the experiment. The patch was made
against application source at Git revision
`52ea91b878ebd254f22b19555835932613b63197`; the original
`src/xai_rag/explainability/faithfulness.py` had SHA-256
`d26495e4e23ccfaebcebc8aa5f6922c47fa52b430b6cccca65175bdd20302c63` when
the snapshot was made. Recheck the current source before attempting a future
experiment: the patch is tied to that version, not a portable upgrade.

The candidate retained NLI and the independent judge and tried a bounded,
small-batch window fallback for one cited chunk when full-chunk NLI rejected
or exceeded the model token limit. It also applied a contradiction veto and
withheld a result if any declared cited source was missing. A reviewer found
that an earlier version could discard a genuine contradiction in another cited
chunk; the final patch prevents this specific multi-source fallback by limiting
it to a single cited chunk. Same-chunk qualifiers and distant negation remain
unresolved. The candidate is not proof that entailment or safety is established.

[baseline-comparison.json](baseline-comparison.json) contains real-model scores
for all 42 previously authored stress cases, with 60 full-premise/window
comparisons generated by [batch-probe.py](batch-probe.py). On this small,
synthetic, unreviewed fixture, the comparison of the original scoring and the
proposed fallback each matched 37/42 expected labels; this is not evidence of
representative quality and does not exercise the complete application.

[candidate-probe.jsonl](candidate-probe.jsonl) contains eight direct-model
observations generated by [candidate-probe.py](candidate-probe.py). The
supported historical E2E claim was scored supported, while the explicit
quoted-refutation and numeric-negation examples were rejected. The model also
accepted two false heading examples and a conditional claim that should not
have been accepted; those failures were also seen in the baseline. The E2E
observation took **111.6 seconds** on a busy CPU, including model setup. Other
cases took 11.3–45.9 seconds. Timings are not a controlled benchmark, but the
observed latency is a material risk given the default query timeout.

The real-model candidate probe was launched before the final patch introduced
four-item microbatches and the single-cited-source restriction. Thus the probe
does **not** validate the exact final patch. Fifteen focused tests passed on an
earlier candidate; after the final two tests were added, the final test rerun
was interrupted during collection because host load and swap usage were high.
The saved [candidate-tests.py](candidate-tests.py) and [candidate.patch](candidate.patch)
are consequently unfinished evidence, not passing final validation.

Before reconsidering a repair, evaluate whole-context and span alternatives on
independently labelled supported, denied, conditional, attributed, long and
conflicting-source evidence; measure false-accept and false-reject behavior and
CPU time; preserve prompt-visible source scope and ensure the independent judge
still runs. Do not infer general safety from an E2E pass or these authored cases.
